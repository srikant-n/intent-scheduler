## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an intimate, tactile desktop companion focused on personal balance through "Intent vs Reality" (Planned vs Actual time logging). The experience rejects corporate metric-driven rigidity in favor of humane reflection, calm productivity, and artisanal digital craftsmanship. It bridges the organic comfort of physical paper journals (like Midori or Hobonichi planners) with modern desktop fluidity.

Target audience: Creative professionals, deep knowledge workers, and mindful individuals who value intentional pacing over enterprise throughput.

### Visual Style: Warm Tactile Minimalism & Fluent Mica
- **Physical Warmth**: Built on natural paper-adjacent foundations—bone, warm linen, unbleached cotton, and warm ink text.
- **Fluent Subtlety**: Native Windows 11 Mica-inspired backdrop tinting, seamless chrome, rounded dialogs, and subtle acrylic layer stacking.
- **Expressive Micro-details**: Hand-stamped badge feels, soft-toned pastel washes for categories, and rhythmic visual dual-tracks that present planned vs. logged blocks without guilt or alarmist error-states.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unhurried, welcoming workspace. Pure stark whites (#FFFFFF) are reserved for foreground card elevatory planes, suspended over a warm canvas of bone linen (#F8F7F4). Ink tones avoid sterile digital blacks, settling on warm stone charcoals.

### Functional Category Colors (Washes & Inks)
- **Primary / Brand**: Terracotta Persimmon (`#EA580C`) - Used for focus states, active timers, and primary action affordances.
- **Deep Learning**: Iris Indigo (`#6366F1` text/stroke, `#EEF2FF` wash).
- **Health & Vitality**: Matcha Green (`#10B981` text/stroke, `#ECFDF5` wash).
- **Creative & Projects**: Sunset Amber (`#F59E0B` text/stroke, `#FEF3C7` wash).
- **Life & Errands**: Soft Coral (`#F43F5E` text/stroke, `#FFF1F2` wash).
- **Rest & Spontaneous**: Lavender Plum (`#8B5CF6` text/stroke, `#F5F3FF` wash).

### Neutral & Canvas Spectrum
- **Canvas Base**: `#F8F7F4` (Warm Linen)
- **Mica Overlay Tint**: `rgba(248, 247, 244, 0.82)` with 32px backdrop blur
- **Surface Layer 1**: `#FFFFFF` (Solid Card Surface)
- **Border Default**: `#EAE6DF` (Subtle boundary line)
- **Border Strong**: `#D6D0C7` (Active dividers / outlines)
- **Text Main**: `#1C1917` (Warm Ink)
- **Text Muted**: `#78716C` (Warm Stone)
- **Text Subtle**: `#A8A29E` (Warm Sand Grey)

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans delivers balanced geometric clarity softened by gentle, friendly curves. To ensure time readouts, daily schedules, and time durations never shift UI alignments during tracking, all numeric representations must enable `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`.

- **Headlines**: Set in weight 600 or 700 with subtle negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to anchor daily views and summary overviews with quiet confidence.
- **Body Text**: Tuned for effortless readability in task titles and journal notes with generous line-heights.
- **Labels & Badges**: Small caps and micro tags use bold weights with slight positive tracking (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`) to simulate tactile ink stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around a native Windows 11 single-window desktop frame:

1. **Integrated Title Bar (38px height)**: Holds the window drag region, unobtrusive SQLite offline persistence badge, sync beacon, and standard Fluent caption controls (minimize, maximize, close) seamlessly embedded into the linen backdrop.
2. **Sidebar Pane (240px - 280px fixed-fluid width)**: Houses collections, backlog inbox, calendar pickers, and view switches.
3. **Dual-Track Main Timeline (Flex fluid)**: Split horizontally into two harmonious columns:
   - **Intent (Planned)**: Left track, delineated by soft dashed structural guide wires.
   - **Reality (Actual)**: Right track, populated by filled, tactile duration blocks.
4. **Persistent Floating Dock (Bottom centered)**: Houses the active recording timer capsule and quick-entry log input, elevated cleanly above timeline scroll views.

Breakpoints conform to desktop window tiling:
- **Compact Window (< 860px)**: Collapses the sidebar to an overlay rail; collapses Dual-Track into a toggle or unified comparative column.
- **Standard Desktop (860px - 1440px)**: Sidebar locked; two-column Intent vs Reality view side-by-side.
- **Wide Desktop (> 1440px)**: Contextual metadata inspection panel docks to the right edge.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, sterile drop shadows. Depth is created through ambient surface contrast, Mica transparency, and warm diffuse under-tints:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F8F7F4` background with native Windows Mica grain effect letting desktop wallpapers bleed warmth subtly through the window.
- **Level 1 (Card & Timeline Surfaces)**: Solid `#FFFFFF` planes bordered by a hairline `#EAE6DF` stroke. No shadow when resting; a micro-shadow `0 2px 6px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.03)` when hovered.
- **Level 2 (Floating Controls & Floating Dock)**: Acrylic glass tint (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with 16px blur) bounded by `#D6D0C7`, casting a soft warm shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Context Menus)**: `#FFFFFF` surface with warm perimeter occlusion: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

Shapes emulate soft-cornered notebook cards and tactile wooden tokens:
- **Standard Containers & Cards**: `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) to match modern Windows desktop geometry.
- **Sheets & Large Panels**: `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for major workspace transitions.
- **Interactive Badges, Pills & Time Floating Dock**: Fully rounded capsules (`9999px`) to invite clickability and physical touch.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Terracotta background (`#EA580C`), crisp white text, pill or 8px rounded corners. Slight inward 1px translate on press.
- **Secondary Action**: Warm bone wash (`#F0EDE6`), ink text (`#1C1917`), no harsh border.
- **Ghost / Tool**: Transparent background with soft `#EAE6DF` hover fill and subtle scale feedback.

### 2. Timeline Blocks (Intent vs Reality)
- **Planned Block (Intent)**: Dashed border (`1.5px dashed #D6D0C7`), transparent background with pale pastel wash on hover. Represents an aspiration without visual guilt.
- **Tracked Block (Actual)**: Solid pastel wash card matching category hue (e.g., `#EEF2FF` for Learning), 1px stroke of the solid category color (`#6366F1`), pill category label, and precise tabular time stamp.

### 3. Floating Bottom Timer Capsule
- Floating pill widget centered 24px above bottom window margin.
- Translucent white acrylic body (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` + blur), hairline border (`#EAE6DF`).
- Live blinking terracotta indicator dot, live tabular counter (`00:42:15`), active task text, and integrated tactile stop/play button.

### 4. Category Badges & Tags
- Hand-crafted look: 20px height, fully rounded pill.
- Muted pastel background matching its taxonomy with saturated ink text (e.g., `#ECFDF5` background with `#047857` text for Health).

### 5. Input Fields & Quick-Log Bar
- Clean horizontal flat inputs with no heavy borders. Uses warm stone placeholder text (`#A8A29E`).
- Focus state softens into an active 1.5px terracotta or iris halo (`rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.2)`).
- Natural language parsing visual indicators: typing `#` triggers category auto-fill chips; typing `@` sets duration tags.

### 6. Window Title Bar & SQLite State Indicator
- Custom height (38px), blending directly into background.
- Left-aligned title with tiny persistent SQLite indicator: a quiet stone pebble icon with tooltip "Local DB: Synced & Saved to disk".
- Fluent native minimize, maximize, and close glyphs flush right.
